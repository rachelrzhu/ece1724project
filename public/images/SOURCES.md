## USDA covers and recipe inspiration

The U.S. Department of Agriculture's Food and Nutrition Service photographed these dishes for its MyPlate cultural food collection. The agency's descriptions inspired the demo dishes. The ingredient amounts and instructions were written for this project. USDA marks each original photograph as public domain. The local JPEGs are resized copies. [Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/)
